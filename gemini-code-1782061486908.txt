### How to Apply:
1. **Save this file** as `templates/view_csv.html`.
2. **Restart Apache:** `sudo systemctl restart apache2`
3. **Hard Refresh your browser:** Press `Ctrl` + `F5` (or `Cmd` + `Shift` + `R`).

This will now fetch the data in the background and prevent your browser from freezing during the load!
